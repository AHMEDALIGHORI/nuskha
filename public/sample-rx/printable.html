Nuskha test prescriptions

Four practice prescriptions for testing the photo reader. These are test sheets only. They are not real prescriptions.

  1. Print on A5, or on A4 at "fit to page". Ordinary paper is fine.
  2. Photograph each sheet with your phone, in daylight, with the whole page in the frame.
  3. Upload the photo in Nuskha with "Scan a prescription".
  4. Compare the result with the answer key on the last page.

Handwriting gets harder from sheet 1 to sheet 4. If you cannot print, you can also photograph this screen.
